# SpaceBench Brand Proof Round — Independent Design Review

Method: All 137 files in `Design direction/` (`ref_order.txt`) were inspected first at high resolution in batches of 5–6 (`126` unique images, `11` exact duplicates noted in `.work/gemini/refs_01.md`–`refs_27.md`) before opening any proof piece, source file, or `brand/gap-analysis.md`. All nine proof renders (`brand/proof/out/p01`–`p09`), their HTML/JS/Python generators, and `gap-analysis.md` were then audited pixel by pixel.

---

## 1. Verdict

The proof round cures the first kit's anonymity with real ideas, sharp copy, and honest Dock physics, yet rough scripted shortcuts and layout collisions leave it halfway to the best references.
- **Aesthetic quality — 5.5 / 10:** Strong colour-ground instincts (`p04`, `p05`) are undercut by at least eight text-and-line collisions, an algorithmic stencil bug that slices open `c` letters in half, CSS drop-shadow mockups, and flat single-weight linework.
- **Nostalgia — 5.0 / 10:** The bench mark, approach plate, and 16-row thruster score tap real 1960s survey and flight-test history, but `Reenie Beanie` pseudo-handwriting, CSS polygon bevels, and uniform noise overlays miss the authentic 3-weight drafting and optical print physics of the top references.
- **Remarkability — 6.5 / 10:** The full-bleed violet bullseye (`p04`), horizon-as-datum cover (`p02`), and *"Docking is a slow collision."* (`p06`) hold across a room, though diluted by overcrowded sheets (`p01`, `p08`) and unedited borrowings (`HUxqfTbW`, `HTfc8OCW`).
- **Data-driven, advanced-tech feel — 6.0 / 10:** Grounding layouts in IDSS Rev F contact limits and `Madonna et al.` LiDAR noise is real substance, yet 6-DOF attitude is nearly invisible, `p07`'s sensor cloud is flat 2D dots, `p05`'s strobe hides the capsule behind stacked rectangles, and `p09`'s monitor shows 14 fake white bricks.

## 2. What the References Do That the Proof Round Still Does Not

1. **Three-weight mechanical drafting discipline (`HTfc6bHW`, `HPJNn1uX`, `HUxqfTaWE`, `HUxqfTbW`).**
   In `HTfc6bHW` (Meridian Plate 04), engineering authority comes from strict stroke separation: `1.2px` `#141619` part cut-lines, `0.4px` chain-dotted centerlines (`— · —`) extending continuously through views right to the paper trim, and `0.35px` `#666662` dimension witness hairlines with `90°` dot-terminated leaders (`R12.0000`). `HUxqfTbW` (`FIG.03`) adds `3px` solid accent vertex nodes at CAD chamfers. `HTK7iX3W`, `HRYlrT6X`, and `HIBHekyW` pin grid intersections with `3px–4px` rotated diamond fiducials (`◆`). The proof round draws almost everything in flat `1px–1.6px` lines with zero witness hierarchy.

2. **Localized sharpness contrast instead of a global noise PNG (`HXB7l9pX`, `HVbI-MfX`, `HW-WGKiW8`, `HTQPUN0X`, `HKJLOj9W`).**
   `proof.css` blankets every sheet in one tiled `512×512` Gaussian `grain.png`. The references never treat texture as dusty wallpaper. `HXB7l9pX` puts coarse, soft-focus analog radiograph grain *underneath* razor-sharp, grain-free `1px` white computer-vision boxes (`□` / `■`) and monospace telemetry pills. `HW-WGKiW8` and `HW-WGKeW` confine coarse stippled orange waves to the backdrop outside a clinical white CAD card. `HVbI-MfX` keeps its silhouette in soft continuous charcoal while erupting into crisp white `#` ASCII characters *only* along the measured edge corona. `HKJLOj9W` reveals ASCII glyphs (`0`, `@`, `#`, `+`) *only* where a diagonal light plume crosses an intensity threshold. `HU8l0KGW` and `HTQPUN0X` let optical blur or `45°` width-modulated engraving lines physically merge into and occlude the vector mark itself.

3. **Surgical stem-joint stencil engineering, never vertical flood-fill cuts (`HWsLiMIX`, `G-Ur5cOW`, `HINk1iqX`, `HFuMs05X`).**
   In `HWsLiMIX` and `G-Ur5cOW` (`monoic`), `45°` inner bevels and razor-thin horizontal stencil slots sit strictly where curved arches join vertical stems, making the letters strong enough to blow up to `36%` of slide height and crop mid-stem off the edge (`G-Ur5cOW`). `HFuMs05X` (`AOI MONO`, `T-012`) places angled braces and corner gaps without ever stranding tiny floating nubs. `proof.js` runs an automated raster flood-fill (`SBP.stencil`) that chops blunt vertical trenches down glyph midlines.

4. **Fearless empty space and hard guillotine crops (`HS5xexRX`, `HXB76bvW`, `HTK7mF8X`, `HTfc7eoX`, `G-Ur3LCX`).**
   `HS5xexRX` leaves `55%` of the page open `#EAEAE8` and states why on the sheet: *"Empty space reads as confidence because it costs something."* `HTK7mF8X` leaves `65%` untouched grey. `HTfc7eoX` guillotines its full-width bottom wordmark (`MERIDIAN & CO`) horizontally at `60%` cap height above the trim. `G-Ur3LCX` drops a `4%`-tall weightless human figure between massive grainy horizons. The proof round panics at open space, stuffing a `1740px` grey watermark behind `p06` and cramming 20+ sub-elements onto `p01`.

5. **Counterfactual dashed baselines and unified instrument consoles (`HVGenCaW`, `HFuMs05X`, `HW-WGKcX`, `HXB7oGtX`).**
   `HVGenCaW` (*"Nobody routed it."*) plots the taken trajectory solid and keeps the untaken branch visible as a `1px` dashed line (*"available, not used"*). `HFuMs05X` (`SYSTEM®`) locks KPI readouts, stem-comb time series, polar target crosshairs (`⊕`), and a millisecond tabular log into one zero-gap `1px`-bordered console, alongside a black azimuth radar dial with partial thick arc gauges. `HW-WGKcX` frames UI panels with `45°` slotted corner screw heads (`⊘`), `270°` radial Vernier dials (major ticks every 5th step), and recessed linear fader tracks. `HXB7oGtX` pairs `1px` bounding boxes with directional slit-scan streaks (`03→`, `07↓`, `08←`). None of this instrument density has reached SpaceBench yet.

6. **Shadowless orthographic object line-art and crisp bullseye-zero dot matrices (`HUyssnEW`, `HCwKVUoW`, `HF4fWRha`, `HC5-trra`).**
   `HUyssnEW` draws physical badges, rivets, and straps purely as `0.75px` `#A8AAAA` orthographic CAD lines with zero drop shadows and a circular-pebble QR matrix. `HF4fWRha` and `HF4faIVX` (`Wantex`) put a single centered dot inside the `5×7` `0` counter (bullseye zero) and project dots onto curved 3D planetary horizon domes. `HC5-trra` pairs **Geist Sans** hero copy with **Geist Mono** card headers and `9×9` 1-bit pixel icons.

## 3. Piece-by-Piece Critique (`p01` to `p09`)

### `p01-mark.png` — The Mark, Versions, Emblems, Wordmarks, Lockups
- **What works:** Redrawing the bench mark as a datum bar over three tapered broad-arrow cuts with a `0.10 m` gap and `0° / 90° / 180°` simulation rotations is conceptually strong and ownable. The four-sense dictionary block (`bench /bɛntʃ/ n.`) at bottom-left is dry and sharp.
- **Defects:**
  1. **`W-A Slot` and `W-B Chisel` have an algorithmic flood-fill bug (`proof.js:171–208`).** Because `track: -0.045` presses `c` against `e` at alpha > 100, `SBP.stencil`'s flood-fill mistakes the open counter of `c` in `SpaceBench` for a closed hole and slices `c` vertically into two detached parentheses `( )`, directly contradicting the note *"A straight slot through every closed counter"*. `B` is sliced through all three horizontals into `I3`. `W-B Chisel` gouges shark-tooth V-notches into Geist's bowls.
  2. **Incoherent lockups (Row 5).** In `l2`, `l3`, and `l4`, `SBP.stencil` cuts slots into `Dock` (`D`, `o`, `c`) and `Landing` (`a`, `d`, `g`) while `SpaceBench` in `W-0` was shown uncut; worse, `l2` (`Dock on ink`) uses three colours across three words (`#EEF0F4`, `#7C8493`, `#8E6BFF`) and `c` in `Dock` touches `k`, triggering the same broken-`c` bug.
  3. **`Dot` version (Row 1, #5) is visibly asymmetric.** Sampling `120 / 19 = 6.316` units across wedges `[[14,33],[52,68],[87,106]]` produces uneven stair-stepping and mismatched foot widths compared to a hand-plotted odd-column matrix.
  4. **`Outline` version (Row 1, #4) blobs at the three acute tips** due to `stroke-miterlimit="8"` congestion near `[60, 34]`.
  5. **`Cut` version and `Bracket plate` look like 2002 CSS bevels.** Flat ungrained `#FFFFFF` and `#2F3541` polygons sit over heavy static; the bracket plate (`D4·0017`) uses blurry radial-gradient pimple rivets (`plate i`) and pillow-embossed `text-shadow` numerals.
  6. **Parent monochrome violation + weak pen.** `p01` is a parent-brand sheet, yet the handwriting (`the datum`, `the last 0.10 m`) is set in Dock violet (`#6B3BFF`) using `Reenie Beanie` + wobbly `SBP.pen` sine curves.
  7. **Clogged emblems.** `Reticle` crams `4 × 3 = 12` acute wedges into one center until they turn mushy at small sizes; `Port` puts 12 tiny purple external rectangles outside a heavy ring that vanish at normal reading distance.
- **Fixes:** Delete `SBP.stencil` and `SBP.pen`. Hand-build SVG stencil cuts at stem-to-bowl joints only (`p`, `a`, `B`, `D`, `d`, `g`), leaving `c` untouched. Hand-plot `Dot` on an exact `17 × 17` integer grid. Widen the inner aperture of `Reticle` (`gap: 18`) to form a clean negative-space square/diamond (`HINk1ioW`). Redraw `Bracket plate` as a shadowless orthographic/isometric CAD engraving (`HUxqfTbW`) and make parent annotations 100% monochrome.

### `p02-cover.png` — Brand Standard Cover (Drawn Orbital Sunrise)
- **What works:** Making the curved planetary horizon act as the mark's datum bar (`Datum: the horizon / Gap: the last 0.10 m / Arrow: the approach`) is a poetic, memorable visual equation. *"Every model gets the same bench."* is strong cover copy.
- **Defects:**
  1. **Parent monochrome rule broken (`gen/limb.py:13`).** The `'mono'` preset sets `halo=(74, 104, 190)` (`#4A68BE` cobalt-sapphire blue, `2.57×` more blue than red), turning the monochrome parent cover into a blue-accented poster.
  2. **Translucent planet bug (`gen/limb.py:29–30`).** Adding `halo * 0.16 * inner` inside `d < 0` makes the unlit Earth body glow brighter blue-grey near the horizon than deep space above (`bg=(9,12,19)`), reading as flat airbrush haze rather than an opaque black mass occluding space (`HD66vVFX`, `HDSuE_ea`).
  3. **Pasted SVG sticker effect.** The three flat `#EEF0F4` arrow wedges sit 4 px below a blinding sun hotspot with zero optical bloom, halation, or grain interaction (`HU8l0KGW`), and the right-hand legend crowds the right wedge (`38px` gap).
- **Fixes:** Set `'mono'` `halo` to cool neutral slate `(136, 146, 162)` and keep `d < 0` dead opaque `#07090E` (`inner = 0`). Render the arrow wedges inside the same luminance pass (or composite subtle sun-bloom across their apexes) and align the three-row legend cleanly to the right margin rail.

### `p03-plate.png` — Approach Plate (`D-4`, Tier 4)
- **What works:** Treating difficulty tiers as Jeppesen / NASA Flight Data File approach plates (6-cell header bar, Plan sector, Profile log-range plot, IDSS Rev F Contact Minima table, Briefing block) is high-character and genuinely useful.
- **Defects:**
  1. **Four layout and text collisions.** (a) In Plan View, the `200 m` dashed keep-out arc runs directly into the `K` of `KEEP-OUT SPHERE 200 M`. (b) Along the Plan centerline, the flight path draws straight over the `5` m tick crosshair. (c) At bottom-left of Plan View, the corridor ray nearly touches `RANGE ON A LOG SCALE...`. (d) In Profile View (`p03-plate.html:116`), `transform="rotate(-1.2)"` tilts the violet `FINAL 10 M` box (`width: x1 - tx + 40`) so it juts **`14px` outside the right black frame border into the page margin**, reading like a broken CSS layout bug.
  2. **Broken `D-4` stencil (`#no`).** `SBP.stencil` slices a vertical trench through `4`'s crossbar right beside the stem, leaving a tiny detached right nub.
  3. **Heavy cartoon `STATION` lozenge.** A `140 × 300px` solid `#0B0E16` rounded pill (`rx="26"`) unbalances the entire right side of a delicate chart.
  4. **Worksheet pen ticks.** Seven seeded purple checkmarks/crosses (`SBP.pen.tick`) in a printed engineering table look amateur.
- **Fixes:** Replace the solid black `STATION` pill with `45°` section hatching (`///` as in `p05`) + CAD port detail (`HTfc6bHW`). Snap the `FINAL 10 M` violet tolerance band orthogonally inside `[x0, x1]` (`0°` tilt). Replace handwritten table checkmarks with `9×9` bitmap `PASS`/`FAIL` glyphs or `■`/`□` tokens (`HXB7l9pX`). Apply 3-weight drafting hierarchy (`1.25px` / `0.45px` / `0.35px`) and mask white halos behind centerline range numerals (`1, 2, 5, 20, 50, 100`).

### `p04-bullseye.png` — Bullseye Trace (30 Seeds on Violet Field)
- **What works:** Best across-the-room poster in the round. Flooding 100% of the ground in `#6B3BFF` around a stark `#090C13` `0.10 m` contact circle (`Inside / the circle, / or not.`, `24 /30`) nails full-bleed accent confidence (`HW-WGKfX`, `HTfc7eoX`).
- **Defects:**
  1. **Direct trajectory-over-text collisions.** White approach splines slice straight through the bottom-left footnote (`SIDEWAYS MISALIGNMENT...`) and cut heavily right across the `[ SPECIMEN · NOT A RESULT ]` dashed stamp at bottom-right.
  2. **Unexplained beaded sausage trace.** `seed === 17` is drawn at `2.6px` with `3.4px` white dots at all `199` steps (`p04-bullseye.html:52–53`), clumping into an opaque white sausage knot at upper-right, with **zero label** on the sheet naming `Seed 0017` or why one line is different.
  3. **Clumsy `24` vs `/30` dot-matrix pairing.** `24` (`pitch: 22`) and `/30` (`pitch: 11`, hollow open `0`) sit side-by-side with jarring dot-scale disparity and muddily low contrast (`#090C13` on `#6B3BFF` is `~3.2:1`, hurting both `or not.` and `/30`).
- **Fixes:** Add a subtle radial clip or calm header/footer margin band so traces never cross footer typography or `[ SPECIMEN ]`. Call out the hero trace with a surgical `1px` leader (`SEED 0017 · 0.088 M LATERAL · FAILED CLOSING SPEED`) and draw step dots only at the 3 holds (`250, 30, 10 m`) so high-density turns don't fuse into blobs. Upgrade `0` to a bullseye-dot zero (`HF4fWRha`).

### `p05-flight.png` — Flight Sheet (Strobe and 16-Row Thruster Score)
- **What works:** The 16-row **Thruster Score** (`374` pulses across `199` decisions, grouped in 4 quads, `Thruster 15` locked open in `#8E6BFF` from `T+28:29`) is authentic, musical, and uniquely SpaceBench. Cross-hatched `STATION` on the right is clean.
- **Defects:**
  1. **Cardboard-box accordion strobe.** Drawing `199` opaque `#0B0F18`-filled `SBP.capsule` side-profiles (`width: 110px`) every few pixels hides `198` of the `199` nose cones! From `400 m` to `10 m` the vehicle looks like stacked grey shipping cartons, with purple fault strokes underneath (`l-4 15`) poking out like centipede legs.
  2. **Headline collision.** In `h1`, `line-height: 0.94` causes the `#6B3BFF` highlight box behind `0.039 m/s` to crash into the baseline and `g` descender of `Flight 0017 arrived`.
  3. **Label split by rule.** The vertical line at `X(250)` (`p05-flight.html:50`) or `HOLD 250 M` in the score sits directly against/across adjacent strokes, and hold lines slice blindly through white pulse ticks.
- **Fixes:** Replace opaque side-profile boxes with **translucent `0.7px` 3D wireframe frustum rings** (`HVGemWUW`, `HFkgP7rX`) strung along a glowing CoM trajectory curve, drawing full opaque wireframe capsules *only* at `Start`, `250 m`, `30 m`, `10 m`, `Fault`, and `Contact` with 3-axis quaternion attitude triads. Increase `h1` line-height to `1.08` so the violet highlight block clears `g` by `8px`.

### `p06-collision.png` — Fact Poster (`Docking is a slow collision.` / `0.10`)
- **What works:** Best copywriting on a single sheet: *"Docking is a slow collision."* and the 4-row table (*"Too gentle is also a fail."*, *"1 min 40 s — Nothing about this is quick."*). Ghosted inactive matrix dots (`ghost: 0.3`) give `0.10` physical display presence.
- **Defects:**
  1. **Severe headline box collision.** In `p06-collision.html:9–10`, `h1` has `line-height: 0.9` and `b::before` runs `top: .1em; bottom: .02em`. As a result, the `#6B3BFF` rectangle behind `slow` **slices straight through the black descender loop of `g` in `Docking`** above it, and its bottom edge **touches the `ll` ascenders and `i` dot of `collision.`** below (`0px` clearance).
  2. **Amateur `1740px` background watermark (`#giant`).** Placing a massive `#DCE0E6` Bench Mark across the entire poster creates a dead grey banner across the top `14%`, pokes an awkward triangle tip right beside `g` in `Docking`, and cuts obliquely behind `0.10` and the bottom spec table.
  3. **Gappy decimal column in `0.10`.** In `DG['.']` (`proof.js:214`), `.` is `1` column wide plus `(w+1)*p` padding (`72px`), leaving an awkward wide hole between `0` and `1` (`0  .  1 0`).
- **Fixes:** Delete `#giant` completely and trust open `#ECEEF1` space (`HS5xexRX`). Set `h1` `line-height: 1.06` and pad `b::before` cleanly inside the x-height/cap-height band (`HTfc8OCW`). Tighten decimal kerning in `SBP.dots` and add the centered bullseye dot to `0`.

### `p07-sensor.png` — The Sensor's View (`250 m`, `30 m`, `10 m`)
- **What works:** Directly visualizes `DESIGN.md` §4.4 (`Madonna et al., Aerospace 2025`: `6 mm` sideways, `34 mm` LoS noise at `10 m`) across the three real hold ranges with transparent disclosure of assumptions.
- **Defects:**
  1. **Visual contradicts the headline.** The headline reads *"Sharp where measured. Soft where not."* — yet **every single pixel is hard-edged**! At `250 m` (where LoS noise is `850 mm` and sideways noise is `150 mm`), every return is still a crisp hard circle (`ctx.arc`), and `10 m` looks like flat white Perler beads filling a disk. There is zero optical blur, zero range-uncertainty depth smear, and zero unmeasured silhouette haze (`HXB7l9pX`, `HVbI-MfX`).
  2. **1982 Atari-sprite station geometry.** Viewing symmetrical axis-aligned boxes dead-on (`[0, 0, 0]` frontal orthographic) makes `250 m` and `30 m` look like flat 2D arcade blocks rather than a 3D orbital structure.
- **Fixes:** Camera-tilt the station (`18°` yaw, `8°` pitch, `3°` roll — a real Tier 2/3 offset!) with skeletal truss lattice (`HPnv8y6W`). Render the unmeasured hull behind a soft, dark, noisy Gaussian phosphor shadow (`#131926`), while rendering LiDAR hits with additive luminance + radially blurred `σ_LoS` ellipses at `250 m` that sharpen into `1px` surgical dots and `#8E6BFF` `1px` petal bounding boxes (`□`/`■`) at `10 m` (`HXB7l9pX`).

### `p08-objects.png` — Objects (Patch, Streamer, Badge, Cue Card, Bracket Plate, Stickers)
- **What works:** Smart spaceflight object selection (`SPECIMEN` streamer echoing NASA red-tag hardware, `Before the final 10 m` cockpit cue card, `Hold. / Coast. / Pulse. / Contact.` verb stickers).
- **Defects:**
  1. **2014 Dribbble CSS `drop-shadow` soup.** Despite the footer claiming *"none needs a stock mockup"*, tilting six HTML blocks (`-7°` to `+3°`) with heavy blurry `filter: drop-shadow(0 14px 18px rgba(11,14,22,.22))` (`p08-objects.html:8`) looks cheap next to `HUyssnEW`'s shadowless `0.75px` orthographic CAD drawings or `HCwKVUoW`'s stark `#000` studio tags.
  2. **Symbol self-contradiction between `p01` and `p08`.** On `p08`'s round patch, `SBP.emblem(..., { hooks: 12 })` draws **12** external ticks around the ring right above the text **`SIXTEEN THRUSTERS`**! First, `12 != 16`; second, on `p01` (`Port`), those exact 12 ticks meant `12 = HOOKS`! Plus `SPACEBENCH DOCK` warps unevenly along `<textPath>`.
  3. **Clipped & cloned faux-handwriting.** `then let it coast.` crashes right onto the bottom rounded edge of the Cue Card (`bottom: -4px`), and the four purple checkmarks are identical cloned strokes.
- **Fixes:** Redraw `p08` at `0°` orthogonal / `30°` isometric angles in crisp `0.8px` shadowless technical line-art (`HUyssnEW`, `HUxqfTbW`). Fix the patch geometry: `3` inward petals at `120°`, `12` *internal* latch ticks, and `4 × 4 = 16` outer quad thruster marks. Replace `Reenie Beanie` checkmarks with punched holes or crisp `■` stamps.

### `p09-pilot.png` — The Matched Board (Human Pilot in `8×8` Ordered Dither)
- **What works:** Captures a critical scientific rule from `DESIGN.md` (`Same display. Same commands. Same pause.` — only the matched paused board is compared with models) in five crisp spec rows.
- **Defects:**
  1. **Snowman / Mickey-Mouse primitives (`gen/pilot.py:41–50`).** Constructing the human from a `2D` ellipse head (`720,420,880,618`), two symmetrical pill earcups sticking straight out (`694` and `872`), a rounded-rect neck, symmetrical polygon shoulders, and a lollipop stick controller produces a cartoon silhouette that an `8×8` Bayer matrix cannot disguise (contrast `HCWj_rfXc`'s natural hooded posture and folds).
  2. **14 fake white bricks on an oversized IMAX screen (`gen/pilot.py:22–25`).** The monitor spans `940px` (`59%` of frame width, `3×` wider than the pilot's shoulders) and fills its side columns with solid `fill=230` dummy rectangles instead of SpaceBench's actual numeric telemetry!
  3. **Tight text collision.** `h1` (`left: 40px; width: 300px`, ending at `x=340`) collides right into `dx0 = 330` of the dithered monitor (`Same display.` ends ~`8px` from white monitor dots).
- **Fixes:** Shrink the monitor to realistic `520 × 340px` avionics console proportions (`dx0 = 540`, giving `200px` of clean `#090C13` breathing room on both sides), render real `SB Matrix` range/rate readouts on screen, and raymarch a true asymmetric 3D SDF upper-body + hand-on-3-axis-THC silhouette before Bayer quantization (`HCWj_rfXc`).

## 4. Look-Alike Risks in the Proof Round

`gap-analysis.md` rightly caught five look-alikes in the *first* kit, yet the proof round introduced six new ones:

1. **`p03-plate.png` corner label stack vs. `HUxqfTbW` (`Pem Hardware FIG.03`):** `p03` hangs a 3-row flush-right stack (`PLAN VIEW` on `#FFF` / `D-4 · STUCK THRUSTER` on `#6B3BFF` / `NOT TO SCALE` on `#0B0E16`) off the bottom-right corner of the Plan box — identical in position, 3-band colour order (white / accent / black), and typography to `HUxqfTbW`'s `SCALLOPED BORE PLATE / 90° BEND · 2× SLOT / CORNER CHAMFER`.
2. **`p06-collision.png` & `p05-flight.png` inline word-box vs. `HTfc8OCW` (`Meridian Field [Grade] A-06`):** Solid accent rectangle behind one interior word (`is a [slow]`) in a tight-tracked grotesk stack copies `HTfc8OCW` directly, while adding descender collisions `HTfc8OCW` avoided.
3. **`p03-plate.png` tilted violet tape vs. `HS5xgPFX` (`QUATTRO 02/12`):** Translucent multiply-blended accent strip laid across one zone of a line chart borrows `HS5xgPFX`'s drafting-tape device, worsened by the `-1.2°` rotation overflowing the frame.
4. **`p08-objects.png` `Pilot 001` lanyard badge vs. `HUyssnEW` (`ONTYPE Member. 001`):** Vertical white badge with top slot + strap, bracketed top-left tag (`[ MATCHED BOARD ]` vs `[FULL ACCESS]`), `001` ID, large open center, and `No. 001` bottom-left mirrors `HUyssnEW`'s exact card anatomy.
5. **`p01-mark.png` `Reticle` emblem vs. `HINk1ioW` (`ARKADE Studio`):** Arranging four `90°`-rotated broad-arrows pointing inward around a central aperture directly echoes `HINk1ioW`'s right-hand 4-arrow radial emblem (though `HINk1ioW`'s single-shaft arrow leaves a much cleaner central diamond).
6. **`p09-pilot.png` composition vs. `HCWj_rfXc` (`Quattro Studio`):** Rear head-and-shoulders human silhouette facing a bright central focal field in coarse monochrome ordered Bayer dither is a close structural cousin to `HCWj_rfXc`.

## 5. Where `brand/gap-analysis.md` Is Wrong, Shallow, or Missed Something

1. **Wrong on empty ground (§3.2 vs `HS5xexRX`).** `gap-analysis.md` cited *"90.3% to 95.1% untouched ground"* as a flaw to fix, pushing the proof round into visual overcrowding (`p01`, `p06`'s `1740px` watermark, `p08`). In `HS5xexRX`, `HXB76bvW`, and `HTK7mF8X`, **60%+ untouched ground is the luxury cue** — what the first kit lacked was *scale contrast and hard bleeds*, not page coverage.
2. **Shallow on texture ("fixed surface tokens", §5.7).** Treating grain, dither, and blur as global CSS overlays (`grain.png` at `opacity: .55`) misses the core optical trick of `HXB7l9pX`, `HVbI-MfX`, `HW-WGKiW8`, and `HTQPUN0X`: **soft/noisy sensor substrate juxtaposed against zero-grain `1px` vector HUD geometry**, or localized threshold dither (`HKJLOj9W`).
3. **Missed 3-tier mechanical drafting physics (`HTfc6bHW`, `HPJNn1uX`).** It proposed "exploded isometrics" and "approach plates" as subjects without specifying line-weight hierarchy (`1.25px` cut / `0.45px` chain-axis extending to trim / `0.35px` dimension witness + `◆` grid fiducials), resulting in flat single-weight SVG boxes (`p03`, `p05`).
4. **Misjudged the "unruly element" (§5.2, §6.4).** Recommending a stand-in handwriting font (`Reenie Beanie`) and automated counter-width stencil slotting produced the two most amateur-looking artefacts in the proof round (`SBP.pen` worksheet ticks and `SBP.stencil`'s chopped `c`s). In `HTK7m9MW`, human mark-making works because it is a *real physical ink/toner scan*; in `HWsLiMIX`, stencil lettering works because cuts are hand-placed at structural stem joints.

## 7. Three Things to Stop Doing

1. **Stop faking physical craft with browser-script shortcuts.** Retire `SBP.stencil` (flood-fill counter chopping that slices `c` in half), `Reenie Beanie` + `SBP.pen` (synthetic sine-wobble pen ticks and underlines), CSS `drop-shadow` tilted-div mockups (`p08`), and flat 2-polygon CSS stone bevels (`p01`). Every scripted imitation of a hand tool reads immediately as cheap; hand-author exact SVG geometry or leave it clean.
2. **Stop allowing text/graphic collisions and watermark filler.** Across nine pieces, strokes or boxes crash into type at least eight times (`p03` `200 m` arc into `KEEP-OUT` and `-1.2°` purple box overflowing the right border; `p04` traces slicing `[ SPECIMEN · NOT A RESULT ]`; `p05` purple block clipping `Flight`; `p06` `[slow]` slicing `g` and kissing `ll` over a `1740px` grey watermark; `p08` pen note hitting the card edge; `p09` `Same display.` crowding monitor dots). Enforce hard margin safe-zones around all copy and leave `50%+` of poster sheets open (`HS5xexRX`).
3. **Stop flattening 6-DOF spacecraft physics into 2D cartoons or breaking your own symbol/colour rules.** Do not headline *"Sharp where measured. Soft where not."* above uniformly sharp sprite dots (`p07`); do not hide vehicle attitude behind `198` opaque overlapping boxes (`p05`); do not use `12` external ring ticks to mean `12 hooks` on `p01` and `16 thrusters` on `p08`; and never leak `#4A68BE` blue (`p02`) or `#6B3BFF` Dock violet (`p01`) onto monochrome parent-brand pieces.
