bench /bɛntʃ/ n.
1 a table where things are tested. 2 surveying bench mark: a cut in stone that fixes a known height. 3 where the judges sit. 4 where a player sits when not picked to play.

Three cuts that meet at a point, under a line.

Surveyors cut this into walls and bridges to fix a known height. The line is the datum. For SpaceBench the line is the docking plane, the arrow is the approach, and the gap is the last distance before contact.
