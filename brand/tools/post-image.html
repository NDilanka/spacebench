Plain-text signature

For the places a picture cannot go: a display name, a bio, the end of a post. These are ordinary Unicode characters, so every device shows them in its own font.

The half disc turns with the simulation, as the mark does. It is a stand-in and not the mark. A screen reader may read it aloud by its Unicode name, for example "circle with upper half black", so use it once, at the start or the end, and never inside a sentence.
